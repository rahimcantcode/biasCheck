This matched random control tests whether strict selection outperforms random selection at equal training size and class counts. Better strict performance would suggest selection value beyond those factors; similar performance would support, but not establish, a size/composition explanation for the baseline gap. Report OOF metrics per seed, their spread, slices, and CENTER recall against strict selection; retain IDs and probabilities for auditing. Seeds reuse observations, folds have overlapping training sets, and predictions share examples, so neither folds nor seeds are independent replicates. Topics, annotators, label reliability, selection difficulty, and representativeness remain confounded. Results establish neither causality nor generalization beyond this dataset.
