Replacing balanced logistic regression with ComplementNB changes both the estimator and class-weight treatment, confounding attribution. Fixed folds and train-only TF-IDF prevent vocabulary leakage, but repeated development use makes OOF results non-independent evidence. With 115 disputed labels, metrics are unstable and reflect annotation choices; the strict-agreement slice may be easier and unrepresentative. Paired IDs and preserved artifacts support auditing, not generalization. Without untouched validation or an independently labeled, representative test set, this experiment cannot establish independent high accuracy or deployment readiness.
