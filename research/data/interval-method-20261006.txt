Reporting 95% Wilson binomial intervals with exact numerators and denominators improves transparency for raw accuracy, selective accuracy, coverage, nonpolitical false labels, and perclass recall. Missing denominators yield null; abstentions count as failed recall. Label intervals marginal iid diagnostics: neither event/source cluster adjusted nor simultaneous, and not valid independent evidence on reused development data. Provide no macroF1 interval or automatic release gate change; dependence and selection can make reported uncertainty misleading.
