The enhancement is sound: fixed label matrices and per-label counts make disagreement transparent while preserving raw agreement and Cohen’s kappa. Symmetric positive agreement measures shared label assignment, not accuracy; neither reviewer is gold. Apply the same logic to relevance categories and return null for jointly absent labels. Excluding missing/skipped pairs is appropriate, but report their counts because selective missingness can bias agreement. Synthetic tests validate implementation only; without human reviews or gold approval, annotation quality and operational readiness remain unestablished.
