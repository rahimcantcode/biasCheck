Quick labeling rules
  • Author framing: LEFT / CENTER / RIGHT only with sufficient relevant text. CENTER means substantively nonaligned, balanced or centrist framing, not “no policy proposal.”
  • Policy stance (optional): author-endorsed issue position only. NO_EXPLICIT_STANCE is distinct from CENTER and never determines the primary label.
  • Attribution: reported quotations are not automatic author endorsement. Read selection and surrounding narration.
  • Uncertainty: distinguish insufficient context, mixed author-endorsed positions and unclear attribution. Leave ambiguity visible.
